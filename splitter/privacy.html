Splitter Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Splitter shows one unread count per account for the Gmail inboxes and Slack workspaces you connect. It is built so that your data stays on your device. We, the developer, do not run a server and never receive your data.

What Splitter accesses

When you connect an account, you sign in on Google's or Slack's own page and choose what to allow. Splitter asks only for what it needs:

  • Gmail: the number of unread conversations in your inbox, plus your email address, name and profile photo so it can label the account. It cannot read subjects, senders or message contents, and it cannot send or delete email.
  • Slack: the number of unread direct messages, plus your name, photo and workspace name. It cannot read message contents or channels, and it cannot post or change anything.

What Splitter stores, and where

  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Account details (email address, display name, Slack workspace name, photo link) and the latest unread counts are kept in the app's storage on your device, where the Home Screen widget can read them.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Splitter contacts only Google and Slack, directly from your device, to fetch the counts.

What Splitter does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No accounts with us, and no data sold or shared.

Removing your data

Remove an account in Splitter, or delete the app, to erase its data from your device. To also withdraw Splitter's access on the provider's side:

  • Google: visit myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Splitter.
  • Slack: in your workspace, open Settings & administration → Manage apps and remove Splitter.

Google API Services

Splitter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Splitter is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
